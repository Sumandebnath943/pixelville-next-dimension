🏘️ PixelVille — Next Dimension

You are the Founder: walk your village at street level, drive its roads, and rise into the sky to build — the village figures out the rest. Families move in, find work, fall in love, vote, gossip and remember.

🚶 On the street

WASD move · Shift run · Space jump · mouse to look (click the view first)
V first/third person · E look at a building or villager nearby
F get into / out of your car (it's parked at the Lodge) · W/S gas & brake · Space handbrake

🏗️ The Planner

P rise into the Planner and back down · WASD glide · wheel zoom · Q/E or right-drag rotate
Pick from the palette, click to place · R turns the door side · drag roads, rails, rivers & bulldoze
Click any building or villager to inspect · right-click/Esc cancels · Ctrl+Z undo · speeds up to 8×

🎮 Gamepad & touch

Sticks move & look · A jump · X interact · Y car · R3 camera · Start Planner · triggers drive.
On phones: left thumb moves, right thumb looks, buttons for the rest. In the Planner, drag to pan and pinch to zoom.

🛖 Getting started

Open the Planner, drop a house near the highway — a road connects itself and the first family moves in. Add jobs, shops and a school, then come back down and watch the morning commute.
