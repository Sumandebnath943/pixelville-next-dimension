PixelVille Next Dimension

You are the Founder: walk your village at street level, drive its roads, and rise into the sky to build. The village figures out the rest: families move in, find work, fall in love, vote, gossip and remember.

On the street

WASD move · Shift run · Space jump · mouse to look (click the view first)
V first or third person · E a building or villager nearby
F into or out of your car (parked at the Lodge) · W/S gas and brake · Space handbrake
Tab your phone: map and directions, wallet, news, tasks, camera

The Planner

P rise into the Planner and back down · WASD glide · wheel zoom · Q/E or right-drag to rotate
Pick from the palette and click to place · R turns the door side · drag roads, rails, rivers and the bulldozer
Click any building or villager to inspect · right-click or Esc cancels · Ctrl+Z undo · up to 8× speed

Gamepad and touch

Sticks move and look · A jump · X interact · Y car · R3 camera · Start Planner · triggers drive.
On phones: left thumb moves, right thumb looks, buttons for the rest. In the Planner, drag to pan and pinch to zoom.

Getting started

Open the Planner and drop a house near the highway: a road connects itself and the first family moves in. Add jobs, shops and a school, then come back down and watch the morning commute.
