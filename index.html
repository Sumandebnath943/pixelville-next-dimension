Gamepad and touch

Sticks move and look · A jump · X use · Y vehicle · R3 camera · LB photo · View phone · Menu Planner · triggers drive. In any menu the d-pad moves, A presses, B backs out. In the Planner the ring in the middle is your cursor: A places, LB turns the palette's tabs, X pauses.
On phones: left thumb moves, right thumb looks, buttons for the rest (▲▼ climb and descend on the flight deck). In the Planner, drag to pan and pinch to zoom.

Work shifts

The phone's Jobs app (or a workplace's card) starts a shift: firefighter, police, paramedic, taxi, bus, delivery, train driver, pilot, electrician, builder, shop or café, PVTV reporter, farmer.
The card top left says what to do; a tag marks where. E does the work, F takes the work vehicle. In a cab or a cockpit: W/S power and brake, E/Q climb and descend. Clock off for your pay.

Getting started

Open the Planner and drop a house near the highway: a road connects itself and the first family moves in. Add jobs, shops and a school, then come back down and watch the morning commute.
