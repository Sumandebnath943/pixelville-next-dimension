# 🏘️ PixelVille — Next Dimension

The 3D sequel to [PixelVille](https://github.com/Sumandebnath943/pixelville): the same living village — families, minds, elections, crime, fires, festivals, disasters — now at street level.

Walk and drive through your village in first or third person, ride its trains and buses, take a shift as a firefighter or a pilot, talk to villagers who remember you — and rise into the sky to build, just like before.

> **Status:** design phase — no code yet.
