# Model sources

Where every downloaded model in PixelVille comes from: the download list for Milestone 9 (all downloaded on 2026-10-30). Raw downloads go in `assets-src/` (not committed); `scripts/people/build.mjs` and its vehicle and animal siblings turn them into the small files in `public/models/`. Every model here is free for any use, commercial included. **CC BY** models must credit their creator by name.

**The credits themselves are in `public/models/CREDITS.md`** (and the game's Credits screen), written by `scripts/credits.mjs`. That list is the one to trust: its names come from each creator's listing as it stands and from the downloaded files (a few creators have renamed their accounts since this table was made), and it gives the original creator of each model built on someone else's. To add, fix or remove a credit, edit `scripts/credits.mjs` and run `node scripts/credits.mjs`.

**Not used**: two downloads below are left out because they look like assets taken from other games, which their uploaders can't license: *ANIMAL & FOOD | Chicken Model (CS2)* (from Counter-Strike 2) and *An Animated Cat* (from a commercial game). Neither is in `public/models/` or the credits.

## How to download (Sketchfab)

1. Sign in to sketchfab.com (a free account).
2. Open each link below and press **Download 3D Model**.
3. Under *Autoconverted format*, pick **GLB**. If a model has no GLB, pick **glTF** (a zip).
4. Save it, with the name Sketchfab gives it, into `assets-src/vehicles/` or `assets-src/animals/`. No renaming needed.
