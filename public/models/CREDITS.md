# Credits for the models

PixelVille's people, vehicles and animals are built from models their creators shared for anyone to use. Thank you to every one of them.

> Missed a credit, or got one wrong? Made one of these models and want it changed or taken out? Please open an issue at https://github.com/Sumandebnath943/pixelville-next-dimension/issues and we'll put it right.

Every Sketchfab model below is licensed under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) (CC BY 4.0). All of them were changed to fit the game, as each entry says; none of their creators endorses PixelVille. The Microsoft Rocketbox characters are under the MIT licence, whose full text is in [people/LICENSE-Rocketbox.md](people/LICENSE-Rocketbox.md).

## People

- **The villagers and the Founder (45 characters)**: "[Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library](https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox)" by Microsoft, licensed under [MIT](people/LICENSE-Rocketbox.md).
  - Converted for the game: re-rigged onto the game's own skeleton, fewer triangles in three levels of detail, textures resized, clothes made recolourable; weapons left out.
